About Me À propos

I'm Louis, a Cloud and DevOps engineer with over 6 years at the University of Bordeaux’s IT Department. I evolved from a Windows Systems Engineer role to a DevOps Engineer one, managing complex infrastructures and deployments for thousands of users.

My skills cover automation, container orchestration and CI/CD, Linux/Windows systems, Infrastructure as Code, Azure, and Active Directory ecosystem.

Currently enhancing my Cloud and DevOps expertise by pursuing Azure AZ-104 and Kubernetes CKA certifications, while exploring AI solution integration.

Now based between France and Indonesia, I’m looking for remote work opportunities (freelance or full-time employment) in France or internationally. To that end, I’ve simplified my life to enable complete location independence.

My take on AI: I believe it’s important nowadays to be clear about how we approach it. I see it as a powerful assistant, not a magic wand. I always need to read, understand, and verify the results before implementing them. No blind copy-pasting here.

Developed modular PowerShell scripts to synchronize over 60,000 user accounts from OpenLDAP to Active Directory, including account provisioning and lifecycle management with Kerberos authentication integration.

Scripts et modules PowerShell pour synchroniser plus de 60 000 comptes d'utilisateurs d'OpenLDAP vers Active Directory, avec création et gestion du cycle de vie des comptes, en prenant en charge l'authentification Kerberos externe, l'appartenance aux groupes selon des attributs LDAP, et la génération de mots de passe aléatoires.
Tout cela assurant une gestion des identité transparente entre les systèmes.
